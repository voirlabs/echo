Features

Drafts that sound
like you wrote them

The model does the writing, on your own plan. Echo tells it what you’ve done and checks that the result reads like your own samples.

A profile that keeps itself

As you chat, your assistant saves who you are, facts worth citing and samples of your writing. The relevant parts get pulled in each time you write.

Works inside Claude and ChatGPT

An MCP connector with OAuth. Paste what you’re replying to into a chat, and the assistant drafts, checks and saves it to your account.

Drafts the page you’re on

Open the browser extension on a page and paste its one-line prompt into your assistant. Back on the page, one click fills every field. It never sends anything.

Checked against your samples

Echo measures your style from your samples: openings, length, lists, punctuation and AI tells. The checks are deterministic, and the server never calls a model.

How it works

Set up once,
then one prompt per page

Echo never submits a form or sends a message. You click send yourself.

  1. Sign in

    Your own server, one password.

  2. Connect your assistant

    Claude or ChatGPT, over MCP, once.

  3. Copy a prompt from the page

    The extension reads the page. Paste its one-line prompt into your assistant and it drafts every field.

  4. Fill the page

    Back on the page, open the extension and choose Fill.

Self-host

Your writing stays
on your server

A Next.js app, Postgres and a Chrome extension you point at your own server.

  • OAuth 2.1 with PKCE for MCP clients
  • No model calls on the server, so no API bill
  • The extension reads a page only when you open it
